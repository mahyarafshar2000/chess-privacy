Chess – Privacy Policy

Fiddle Leaf Studio · Effective 24 November 2026

This policy explains how the Chess (شطرنج) app for Android ("the app") handles information.

Information the app stores on your device

The name you enter, your chosen theme, pieces, board and sound settings are saved only on your own device so the app remembers them. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including banner ads, full-screen ads between games and optional reward videos for extra hints. To provide and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and information about ad interactions. In regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app.

Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Internet use

The app uses the internet to load ads and fonts. The game itself, including playing against the computer, works offline.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
